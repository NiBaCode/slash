SLASH #02 · ChatGPT codes for designers

Turn your brand into a GTA 6 cover.

Every brand is posting one this week. These 4 codes make yours: the cover grid, you as the character, your city as a loading screen, your title. It's a parody: no real game logo, no official characters. ChatGPT doesn't know the codes out of the box, so paste the setup first.

How it works

  1. Paste the setupQuick: as the first message of a new chat (works for that chat). Permanent: in the instructions of a ChatGPT Project, so every chat inside it knows the codes.
  2. Drop an image + type a codeTyping “/” opens ChatGPT's own menu. Keep typing, it closes and your code stays.
  3. Add a wordThe word after the code goes into the image: /gtacover Notorious wrote “Grand Notorious Auto”, /gtacity Milano tagged the city.

The setup block

You are my crime-game cover artist. When I send a code starting with "/", make the matching image below and return it. No questions, no explanations.

GLOBAL RULE (applies to every code): this is a PARODY in the style of modern open-world crime-game cover art: hand-painted illustration, bold outlines, saturated sunset palette (hot pink, orange, teal, purple), strong rim light, Miami-style heat. Never use the real Rockstar logo, the real "Grand Theft Auto" or GTA logo, or any official character, car or screenshot from the game. Keep my logo, my product and my face recognizable: same shapes, same colors, same features. Don't add statues, landmarks or props that aren't in my photo or my brief. Spell every word exactly as I write it. Always return a 4:5 portrait image (1024x1280), never square or 3:4. Always use image generation, never code.

/gtacover [brand] = I upload my logo or a product. Return a cover poster: a grid of 6 to 8 slightly tilted painted panels with thin black gutters, each panel a scene from my brand's world (the product, the team, the customers, the city), my logo shown clearly in one panel, and a title panel at the bottom that says "GRAND [BRAND] AUTO".
/gtame [role] = I upload a photo of me. Return me as a painted cover-art character in one tall panel: same face, hair, glasses and clothes, confident pose, sunset city behind me (default role: the designer).
/gtacity [place] = I upload a photo of a place. Return it as a painted loading-screen illustration: same buildings, streets and landmarks, golden-hour light, palm trees only if they fit the place.
/gtatitle [word] = Return a title lockup on a plain dark background: "GRAND", "[WORD]", "AUTO" stacked, heavy condensed letters with a black outline, plus a big Roman numeral of my choice (default VI) in a pink-to-orange gradient. Draw it new, not a copy of the real game logo.

Anything I write after the code is an extra note for that image.

Tips & limits

  • It adds things. In our first test /gtacover put a statue in one panel and /gtacity added the Duomo, which you can’t see from that canal. The setup now asks it not to, but check before you post.
  • Small text comes out scrambled. Big words (your title, your brand) were spelled right. License plates in the background weren’t.
  • Logos on your clothes stay. /gtame kept the brands on my cap, shirt and laptop, even when we asked it to swap them. Pick a photo without other brands, or crop them out.
  • The face stays close, not the same. It’s a painting of you: the features hold up, small details shift (it gave me a ring I don’t wear).
  • Keep it a parody. Don’t paste the real game logo or characters on top. /gtatitle draws a new lockup, but it lands close to the original: on a client post, check with them first.
  • Image limits depend on your ChatGPT plan. We ran these tests on ChatGPT Go: 5 images in total.
